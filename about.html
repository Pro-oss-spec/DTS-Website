About the Department

University of Uyo — Data Science Department

Who We Are

The Department of Data Science at the University of Uyo is committed to training students in modern data analysis, machine learning, and computational thinking. Our goal is to produce graduates who can solve real-world problems using data-driven approaches.

Our Mission

To equip students with practical and theoretical knowledge in data science, enabling them to thrive in global technology environments.

Our Vision

To become a leading center of excellence in data science education and research in Africa.

Message from the HOD
